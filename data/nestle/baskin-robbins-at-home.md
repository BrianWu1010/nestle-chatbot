### Frequently Asked Questions

All BASKIN ROBBINS® ice cream tubs and sandwiches sold in grocery stores are proudly made in Canada* at our London, Ontario factory, using 100% Canadian milk.
*From domestic and imported ingredients.
You can enjoy BASKIN ROBBINS® at home with our 946 ml ice cream tubs, featuring iconic flavours like Gold Medal Ribbon™ and Chocolate Mousse Royale®, along with Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough, Mango Tango and Cotton Candy.
Our ice cream sandwiches are perfect for sharing or enjoying solo. They are available in grocery stores in three delicious flavours: Chocolate Mousse Royale®, C hocolate Chip, and Caramel Ribbon.
BASKIN ROBBINS® ice cream tubs and sandwiches are available at most major retailers across Canada, including Loblaws, Metro, Sobeys and Walmart.
To explore both online and in-store options , select the Products tab in the top banner and click the "Where to Buy" button below the BASKIN ROBBINS® flavour you want! A pop-up will provide options for shopping online or locating stores near you by entering a postal code or city.
Looking for a BASKIN ROBBINS® Scoop Shop? Visit www.baskinrobbins.ca/ for more details and locations.
*BASKIN ROBBINS® is an independent brand. Nestlé Canada manufactures and distributes BASKIN ROBBINS® products under a licensing agreement.
All BASKIN ROBBINS® ice cream tubs and sandwiches are Kosher certified. Please note that our products are not Halal certified.
BASKIN ROBBINS® ice cream tubs and sandwiches are made without any artificial colours. Additionally, select flavours—including Chocolate Mousse Royale® (tub and sandwich), Mango Tango and Cotton Candy are made without any artificial flavours.
All BASKIN ROBBINS® ice cream tubs and sandwiches are made with plant-based mono- and diglycerides.
