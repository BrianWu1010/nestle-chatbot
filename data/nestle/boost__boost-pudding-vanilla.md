## BOOST Vanilla Nutritional Pudding 4-Pack

BOOST Vanilla Pudding is a nutritionally complete oral nutrition supplement. Each 142g serving provides 230 calories and 7 g of protein. Kosher and gluten-free, this ready-to-eat pudding can be consumed as a snack or as a dessert. Be sure to try BOOST Chocolate Pudding, too! BOOST Pudding can be stored, unopened, in a cool dry place. Avoid freezing or high temperatures. Once opened, cover, refrigerate, and use within 24 hours. For oral use only, as directed by a healthcare professional.
