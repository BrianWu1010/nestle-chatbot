## BOOST Chocolate Protein Powder 880g

BOOST Chocolate Instant Breakfast Powder delivers a quick, nutritious start with 15 g protein, 3 g fibre, and calcium plus 16 key vitamins and minerals. Just mix 1/3 cup (40 g) BOOST powder with 1 cup (250 ml) whole milk or add to recipes. The 880 g canister makes 22 servings.
BOOST Powder Chocolate Features and Benefits
- Breakfast in an Instant: BOOST Chocolate Instant Breakfast Powder is a convenient and easy way to enjoy a nutritious breakfast
- Just Add Milk: To prepare, mix 1/3 cup (40 g) of BOOST powder with 1 cup (250 ml) whole milk, or add to your favourite smoothie or breakfast recipe
- Source of Calcium, Protein & Fibre: When prepared as directed, BOOST powder instant breakfast delivers 15 g protein, 3 g fibre, and is an excellent source of calcium
- Supports Immune System: A source of zinc, iron, and vitamins A and C, which contributes to the normal function of the immune system
- Makes 22 Servings: Each 880 g reclosable and recyclable canister of BOOST Powder contains 22 servings
- Kosher: BOOST Chocolate Instant Breakfast is kosher
