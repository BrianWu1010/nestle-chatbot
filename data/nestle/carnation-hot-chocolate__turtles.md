## CARNATION TURTLES Hot Chocolate Mix 400g

CARNATION TURTLES hot chocolate mix will put a smile on your face, even on the chilliest days. A yummy blend of CARNATION hot chocolate with pecan and caramel notes, it's easy to prepare. Just add 3 tablespoons hot chocolate powder to 250mL hot water or warm milk for a creamy-tasting, indulgent cup of hot chocolate perfect for sharing on a cold day at the cottage, around a campfire, or when taking a moment by yourself at home. CARNATION TURTLES hot chocolate mix comes in a convenient 400g canister that makes it easy to scoop, reseal and store. Make your hot chocolate extra special by dipping a caramel syrup-coated mug rim in shaved chocolate pieces, top with whipped cream, and finish with Mini Turtles Milk Chocolate candy. Enjoy CARNATION TURTLES hot chocolate from Canada's #1 hot chocolate brand today!​
