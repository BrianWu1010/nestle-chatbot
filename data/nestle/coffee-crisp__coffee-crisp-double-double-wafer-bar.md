## COFFEE CRISP Double Double Wafer Bar

What's more Canadian than COFFEE CRISP? A Double Double COFFEE CRISP!! Now you can get one in a sweet, coffee, chocolatey snack bar with the NESTLÉ COFFEE CRISP Double Double Wafer Bar (50 g). This sweet and creamy treat is pure Canadian goodness with a harmonious blend of layers of airy coffee crème and light crispy wafers all wrapped in a thin, chocolatey coating. Made with all-natural flavours and no artificial flavours, it's the delightful, go-to snack with a perfectly balanced taste for a morning, afternoon or evening pick-me-up!
