## NESTLÉ BIG TURK 2-Piece Turkish Delight Candy Bar

Surprise your taste buds with something a little different with NESTLÉ BIG TURK 2-piece Turkish Delight Candy Bar (86 g). Made to be shared with a friend, each bite is a delicious combination of Turkish delight, a chewy fragrant jelly centre coated in a thin milk-chocolate shell. This tasty treat is made with natural colours and flavours, and 100% sustainably sourced cocoa. Originally introduced in 1974 under the Smiles N' Chuckles label, BIG TURK has been a classic favourite ever since. This Candy Bar invites you to share this mouth-watering treat with friends and loved ones. After all, something this tasty deserves to be experienced with others.
