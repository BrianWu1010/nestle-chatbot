## BIG TURK Turkish Delight Candy Bar 60g

Surprise your taste buds with something a little different with NESTLÉ BIG TURK Turkish Delight Candy Bar (60 g). Each bite is a delicious combination of Turkish delight, a chewy fragrant jelly centre coated in a thin milk-chocolate shell. This tasty treat is made with natural colours and flavours, and sustainably sourced cocoa. Originally introduced in 1974 under the Smiles N' Chuckles label, BIG TURK has been a classic favourite ever since. This candy bar invites you to share this mouth-watering treat with friends and loved ones. After all, something this tasty deserves to be experienced with others.
