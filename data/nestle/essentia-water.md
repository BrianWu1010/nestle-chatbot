Essentia Water ® is ionized alkaline water that has a pH of 9.5 or higher with a clean, smooth taste.
Essentia Water ® uses a unique, proprietary three-step process. The process begins with purification through reverse osmosis and microfiltration to remove contaminants, followed by the infusion of a mineral blend (i.e. electrolytes) present in trace amounts for taste and to complement the body’s natural mix. Finally, the ionization process removes bitter-tasting ions, which results in a water with a pH of 9.5 or higher with a clean, smooth taste.
It’s actually what we take out, not what we put in that results in Essentia ® ionized alkaline water. Most alkaline waters achieve higher pH from the source (i.e. from minerals that exist based on geographic location and result in higher pH) or from the adding of minerals in (e.g. electrolytes). Essentia ® achieves alkalinity through its proprietary ionization process. This process not only removes bitter-tasting ions, but also raises the pH level to 9.5 or higher and results in a clean and smooth-tasting water.
We use municipal water supplies, which goes through our three-step proprietary process, including in-depth purification, that transforms it into ionized alkaline water with a pH of 9.5 or higher.
