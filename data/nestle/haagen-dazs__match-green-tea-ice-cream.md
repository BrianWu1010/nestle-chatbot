## HÄAGEN-DAZS® Matcha Green Tea Ice Cream

There’s something simply extraordinary about HÄAGEN-DAZS® Matcha Green Tea (450 ml). Authentic Japanese matcha powder from Uji, Kyoto, is blended with the rich creaminess of 100% Canadian dairy. We carefully blend five simple ingredients – cream, skim milk, egg yolks, sugar, and Japanese Matcha tea – to create a delicate, yet full-bodied, flavour. Since 1960, our passion has been to transform the finest ingredients into extraordinary ice cream.

- Authentic Japanese matcha powder from Uji, Kyoto, blended with sweet cream.
