## HÄAGEN-DAZS Vanilla Almond Ice Cream Bars

Indulge in the irresistible taste of HÄAGEN-DAZS Vanilla Almond Ice Cream Bars (3 x 88 ml). Since 1960, our passion has taken us on a journey to transform only the finest ingredients into a simply extraordinary ice cream experience. This divine treat starts with sweet vanilla and fresh cream, blended together to create our smooth vanilla ice cream. Then, our bars are covered in crunchy, roasted almonds and rich milk chocolate. Made with 100% Canadian dairy, each bar is the perfect combination of creaminess and crunch.
