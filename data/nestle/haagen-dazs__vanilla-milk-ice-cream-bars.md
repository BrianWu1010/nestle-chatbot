## HAAGEN-DAZS Vanilla Milk Ice Cream Bars

Proudly Prepared in Canada using 100% Canadian Dairy
Indulge in the irresistible taste of HÄAGEN-DAZS Vanilla Milk Chocolate Ice Cream Bars (3 x 88 ml). Since 1960, our passion has taken us on a journey to transform only the finest ingredients into a simply extraordinary ice cream experience. To create these delicious ice cream bars, we dip our smooth vanilla ice cream in a rich coating made with HÄAGEN-DAZS chocolate. The result is a decadent favourite, made with 100% Canadian dairy.
