## Difficulty Swallowing Materna® Prenatal Multivitamin Tablets

#### Friday, September 03, 2021

If you find our MATERNA Prenatal Multivitamin tablets difficult to swallow, here is what we recommend as options that might help:
- Take your tablet with food.
- Break it in half and consider taking at two different times in the same day (Make sure to have clean, dry hands and keep the remaining half of the pill out of reach of children).
- Crush it and mix the powder into a drink.
If the options above do not work, you may also consider taking our MATERNA Prenatal Multivitamin Gummies + DHA in a chewable gummy format, which makes it easier to swallow.
Note: To help make things even easier on you, you can pick up a pill splitter or pill crusher at your local pharmacy. If you only need to cut your tablet, just make sure you do so along the middle. Do your best to cut the tablet evenly, as sharp edges may be difficult to swallow.
