## Donations and Sponsorship

## Enhancing the quality of life

At Nestlé Canada, our purpose is to enhance the quality of life and ensure a healthier future for all Canadians and supporting thriving communities starts in our own neighbourhoods. That’s why, we like to direct our charitable and non-profit support toward programs and organizations that focus on increasing access to healthy food, promoting community well-being and improving planetary health. We work directly with our key charitable partners including Kids Help Phone, United Way, Food Banks Canada, the Grocery Foundation, Canadian Red Cross and the CEE (Centre for Young Black Professionals). Occasionally, we also sponsor additional events or programs that align to our company purpose, as outlined above.Nestlé Canada is committed to supporting the communities in which we work and live and actively promotes our corporate philosophy Good Food Good Life.
