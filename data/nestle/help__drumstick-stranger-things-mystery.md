## Decode the Mystery Flavour: DRUMSTICK® x Stranger Things™ Mystery Flavour Contest

### Monday, February 23, 2026

Enjoy a taste from another dimension!
DRUMSTICK® is excited to launch a limited time only mystery flavour cone in partnership with Netflix Stranger Things™. Take a guess at the mystery flavour and you could win one of five exclusive prize packs loaded with frozen treats and cool merchandise.
The contest runs from February 23 to July 31, 2026, closing at 11:59 PM EST. To enter, simply visit www.drumstick.ca/mystery during the contest period and submit your mystery flavour guess along with a completed entry form.
There are five contest prizes to be won. Each prize includes:
- One (1) year’s worth of DRUMSTICK® cones to share with friends and family (provided as 52 free coupons redeemable for any variety of DRUMSTICK® 4-count cone boxes)
- One (1) DRUMSTICK® x Stranger Things™ cooler
- One (1) Stranger Things™ Demogorgon figure
- One (1) Stranger Things™ plush
- One (1) DRUMSTICK® hat
- Pair of DRUMSTICK® socks
The approximate retail value of each Prize is $834 CAD. Only selected winners will be contacted by email within 5 days of the draw date.
We wish all participants the very best of luck and can’t wait to see the creative flavour guesses that come our way!
