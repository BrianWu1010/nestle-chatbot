## Nestlé Canada Announces Voluntary Recall of one SKU of GERBER® Arrowroot Biscuits

#### Monday, January 26, 2026

Nestlé Canada is initiating a voluntary recall of one SKU of Gerber® Arrowroot Biscuits out of an abundance of caution due to the potential presence of soft, plastic and/or paper pieces that should not be consumed. The material comes from an arrowroot flour supplier which informed us of a recall. We are no longer working with the flour supplier.
This voluntary recall is isolated to three batches of Gerber® Arrowroot Biscuits 155g products. No other Gerber® products are affected by this recall.
The quality and safety of our products is our highest priority, and we take the responsibility of feeding babies seriously.
Nestlé Canada has not received any complaints related to this issue. We are acting out of an abundance of caution following a recall from the supplier.
Consumers who have purchased affected Gerber® Arrowroot Biscuits should not feed this product to their child and can contact Nestlé Consumer Services at 1-800-387-5536 or contact@ca.nestle.com (Monday to Sunday from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. EST) for reimbursement and then dispose of the product.
