## Gerber® Baby Canada Photo Search Contest

#### Monday, November 3rd, 2025

Get your cameras out... our Canadian Gerber Photo Search Contest 2025 is now open!​
TO ENTER THE CONTEST: Follow @Gerber_Ca on Instagram, then post a photo of your little one your public account with the hashtag #GerberCAPhotoSearch2025 in your caption. In your contest entry post, tell us about your little one by providing a brief description of their bio and how your little one inspires smiles and laughter. You can enter as many times as you’d like! Just make sure each entry features a different photo​! If you’re selected, you’ll be notified through Instagram’s direct messaging feature from the @Gerber_ca Instagram account. The contest runs from November 3 to November 24, 2025, closing at11:59 PM EST. So don’t wait—start snapping and sharing today! Top 10 Finalists and the winner will be revealed between December 12, 2025, and December 19, 2025. ​
PRIZES: There is a total of 11 Prizes available to be won, ranging in value from $100 to $3,300 CAD.​
The grand prize winner will receive a two thousand five hundred Canadian dollar ($2,500.00 CAD) personal cheque to be used towards the purchase of a Registered Education Savings Plan registered in the name of the child depicted in the winning Photo, a curated mealtime essentials box from EZPZ, adorable outfits from Gerber Childrenswear and will receive monthly Gerber gift boxes. The grand prize winner will also be awarded the title of the GERBER® Canada’s 2025 Spokes baby and chief growing ambassador. Top 10 finalists will receive a Gerber gift box valued at $100 CAD. ​
​Let’s celebrate the joy of Canadian babies—one photo at a time. We can’t wait to meet your little one! 💙​
