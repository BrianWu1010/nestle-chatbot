## Nestlé® Good Start® Infant Formula Brand Sold to Perrigo Company

#### Monday, June 16, 2025

We sold our Good Start® infant formula brand to Perrigo Company in Canada and the U.S., in 2022. In Canada, these products include the Good Start® infant formula powder and liquid and Good Grow® powder brands. No other Nestlé®, Gerber®, or Materna® products are part of this transaction.
We understand you may have some questions regarding this transition. For any questions, inquiries, or concerns about your Good Start® product, kindly contact Perrigo Company at 1-800-864-9671 or email them at www.perrigo.com/contact-us .
