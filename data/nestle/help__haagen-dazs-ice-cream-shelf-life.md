## Häagen-Dazs® Ice cream Shelf Life

#### Wednesday, June 04, 2025

For the best enjoyment, we recommend that Häagen-Dazs® be consumed within one month from the date of purchase. Our products can last much longer (up to two years from the manufacture date) if kept frozen at -26 to -29 degrees Celsius, however most household freezers typically only reach -18°C.
