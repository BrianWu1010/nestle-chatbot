## Häagen-Dazs® Tub Size Change

#### Wednesday, June 04, 2025

With the significant increase in the price of commodities over the last few years, we have made the difficult decision to slightly reduce the size of our Häagen-Dazs® tubs from 500 mL to 450 mL, to keep the price consistent. Rest assured, none of our recipes have changed, as we pride ourselves in delivering the best-in-class Häagen-Dazs® product experience that our consumers expect from our brand. We are committed to preserving our recipes using only the purest and finest ingredients.
