## Job Postings and Application Statuses

#### Tuesday, November 11, 2025

The length of time a job posting is active for Nestlé Canada depends on the nature of the position being filled and the unique recruitment process of that position. Typically, we interview two to three weeks after posting a job.
When you apply for a job with Nestlé Canada, you will receive a system-generated email confirming that we received your application. Once you have formally submitted your application, your information will be available in our Applicant Tracking System. Our recruiters will review your resume and assess against the requirements of the position you applied for. You will be contacted directly by someone from our talent acquisition team if you’re a potential fit for the position you applied for.
Due to the high number of applications that we receive, we’re unfortunately unable to contact candidates who aren’t selected to continue onto our interview process. Once the position has been filled, the job posting is taken down.
To follow the progress of your application, click on the Search Jobs button found on https://corporate.nestle.ca/en/careers , click on the job you’ve applied for, and then click on View Profile at the top right of your screen. Once logged into your profile, you will be able to view the status of each completed job application you have submitted.
