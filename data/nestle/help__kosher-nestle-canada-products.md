We’re always striving to meet the needs of our diverse consumer group. In Canada, we only certify products as Kosher if their ingredients, production process and production facility qualify them. To ensure your product is Kosher, check for a kosher symbol on the packaging.
We use the Orthodox Union (OU) and Kashruth Council of Canada (COR) as Kosher certification bodies.
