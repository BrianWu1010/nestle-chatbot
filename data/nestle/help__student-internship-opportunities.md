## Nestlé Canada Internships and Opportunities for Students

#### Friday, January 06, 2023

Nestlé needs YOUth. Nestlé Canada is proud of its Youth Initiative , our multifaceted program designed to help young Canadians fulfill their ambitions and positively contribute to Canadian market growth.
Communities can only thrive if they offer a future for younger generations. Nestlé Canada helps young Canadians fulfill their ambitions and develop professional skills through a range of opportunities. Our internship and entry-level opportunities are posted between August and October.
We’re always looking for great people regardless of their field or level of expertise. For more information on how to apply or to learn more, we encourage you to visit our career website: https://www.corporate.nestle.ca/en
