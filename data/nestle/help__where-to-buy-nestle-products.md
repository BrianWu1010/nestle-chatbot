## Where to Buy Nestlé Canada Products

Wednesday, October 22, 2025
Most of our Nestlé products can be found at any major retailer across Canada.
The WTB (Where to buy) tool has been decommissioned from our site. If you’re interested in buying our products online or in store, you can look on our website, find the product that you’re looking for, and click on the “Where to Buy” button. A pop-up widget will appear and offer both online and find nearby in-store options. Below is a list of retailers that carry our products as well as some of their subsidiary stores:
Loblaw includes: No Frills, Real Canadian Superstore, Shoppers Drug Mart, Atlantic Superstore, Fortinos, Provigo. Sobeys includes: IGA, FreshCo, Foodland. Metro includes: Food Basics.
Please note: We do not have visibility over the retailer's inventory nor the shipment details. The list will show stores that have ordered recently and might have the product on their shelves now, but this is not a guarantee. We recommend calling your local store ahead of time to inquire. If your favourite store stopped carrying a product and the product is not discontinued, you can share your interest with the store manager. Ultimately, retailers choose their inventory and it can be helpful for them to know what their consumers are looking for.
