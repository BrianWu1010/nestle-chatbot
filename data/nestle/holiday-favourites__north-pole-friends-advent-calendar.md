## NESTLÉ North Pole Friends Advent Calendar

Countdown to Christmas with NESTLE North Pole Friends Advent Calendar! Behind each of the 24 doors lies a special Christmas chocolate treat -- a KITKAT Mini Bar, SMARTIES, a KITKAT Bubble, an AERO Mini Bar, or a KITKAT Santa. It's the perfect chocolate gift to kick off the holiday season!
NESTLÉ North Pole Friends Advent Calendar Features and Benefits
- Countdown To Christmas: NESTLE North Pole Friends Advent Calendar is a fun way to countdown to Christmas
- Christmas Chocolate Fun: Behind each of the 24 doors of this advent calendar is either a KITKAT Mini Bar, SMARTIES, a KITKAT Bubble, an AERO Mini Bar, or a KITKAT Santa
- Ready For Gifting: NESTLE North Pole Friends Advent Calendar makes a perfect Christmas chocolate gift to kick off the holiday season
- Rainforest Alliance Certified: NESTLE'S Cocoa Plan (in collaboration with the Rainforest Alliance) is helping cocoa farmers enhance farming practices.
- Proudly Supporting Kids Help Phone: For more than 34 years, NESTLE Canada has raised millions for Kids Help Phone, Canada’s only 24/7, free, confidential, and multilingual e-mental health service
- Loved Worldwide: Over 22 billion KitKat fingers are consumed globally every year
