## KITKAT® STORY

The perfect balance of chocolate and wafer, there's a reason KitKat® is enjoyed in more than 80 countries. The iconic brand is an international symbol for hitting the pause button on life - to enjoy a well-earned break.

### THE WORLD'S FAVOURITE BREAK

From classic fingers to chunky, original to peanut butter, there is a KitKat® for everyone. KitKat® is the first global chocolate brand to use 100% sustainably sourced cocoa through the Nestlé Cocoa Plan and Rainforest Alliance Certification. Meaning there's no reason not to pause… and enjoy a break

#### 1935

THE ORIGINAL
KitKat was first launched in the UK with the name ‘Chocolate Crisp’ – even back then it had the famous four fingers.

#### 1944

THE FIRST VARIATION
During the Second World War, KitKat was made using dark chocolate, and the packaging was blue rather than our iconic red.

#### 1958

BIRTH OF THE BREAK
The famous 'Have a break, have a KitKat' strapline was first used. It's been with us ever since.

#### 1960

HALF THE SIZE, ALL THE TASTE
Now a classic, the first Two-finger KiKat was launched.

#### 2007

KITKAT ICE CREAM
Te first KitKat ice cream is launched, providing a perfect break for a sunny day.

#### 2014

CREATE YOUR OWN KITKAT
The first KitKat Chocolatory is launched in Japan - allowing fans to design their own flavour of KitKat for the ultimate personalized break. Our Australian Chocolatory wasn't far behind.

#### 2018

THE RUBY
The KitKat Ruby Chocolate was launched.

#### 2023

LAUNCHED KITKAT POPS
A new way to enjoy your breaks were introduced - KitKat Pops.
