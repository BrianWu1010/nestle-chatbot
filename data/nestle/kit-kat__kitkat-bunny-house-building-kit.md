## KIT KAT Bunny House Building Kit

This easy-to-assemble NESTLÉ KITKAT Bunny House Building Kit (406 g) has everything you need to create your own magical bunny house. No baking required and complete with milk chocolate wafer KITKAT bars, colourful SMARTIES lentils, decorative sprinkles, and icing. This all-in-one kit even includes step-by-step instructions and assembly trays so your family can focus on wild imaginations, without the need for baking or extensive preparation. Once complete, be sure to share your work of art with the KITKAT-loving community using the hashtag #MyBreak.
