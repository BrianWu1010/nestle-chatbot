## 100 Years of MAGGI®

For over 100 years, MAGGI® has been right there in kitchens around the world, a trusted companion to home cooks who know that great flavour doesn’t have to be complicated.

## How MAGGI® Was Created

In 1884, a young visionary named Julius Maggi had an idea that would revolutionize the world: to create a culinary solution for busy families with no time to cook nutritious meals at home. As more women began working in factories and spending less time at home, he saw a need for delicious, quick and affordable meals.

## In 1886, MAGGI® Liquid Seasoning was Born

After years of research, MAGGI® introduced its first powdered soup, Pea and Bean Soup, which became an instant success. This innovation led to the creation of MAGGI® Liquid Seasoning and Bouillon cubes, pioneering products that spread from Switzerland to homes worldwide.

## Bringing MAGGI® to the World

Nestlé acquired MAGGI® in 1947, uniting their vision to deliver quality, affordable meals worldwide. Today, MAGGI® continues to offer the flavours people love with trusted nutrition and accessible options across the globe.

## MAGGI® Today

Over the last decades, over 4,600 food portions have been prepared with MAGGI® products every second across the globe. Today, MAGGI® products are made to simplify everyday cooking while still leaving room to explore, experiment, and make each dish your own.
