#### Citrus Americano with Nescafé Gold Dark

Awaken your senses with a morning cup of citrus glory.
- Serves: 1
- Cooking Time: 45 minutes
- Preparation Time: 4 minutes
- Total Time: 49 minutes
Featured Product:

##### Ingredients

- 1-2 tsps Nescafe Gold Dark
- 170 ml very hot water (~80°C)
- 2 tsps (8g) brown sugar
- ½ mid-sized orange

##### Equipment

- Mug, to serve

##### Recipe Steps

- Cut half an orange in thin slices.​
- Dust generously each slice with brown sugar for caramelization. Dry the slices in the oven at 120°C/250°F for approximately 45 minutes or until dry.​
- Mix the instant coffee with 170ml of hot water (~80°C), just wait for the small bubbles to appear. No need to boil so you can save on energy). Stir well.​
- Take the oranges out of the oven. Top with the dried orange slices and serve hot.​
- Unlock a new twist to a traditional Americano recipe with NESCAFÉ. Sit back, sip and relax with your very own homemade citrus Americano, NESCAFÉ style. This is definitely a recipe to add to your home barista recipe list.
