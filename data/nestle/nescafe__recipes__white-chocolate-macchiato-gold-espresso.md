#### White Chocolate Macchiato with Nescafé Gold Espresso

Coffee + white choc= show stopping delight. This White Chocolate Macchiato layers smooth Nescafé Gold with creamy white-choc milk and a touch of foam for a café-style treat at home.
- Serves: 1
- Cooking Time: 0 minute
- Preparation Time: 5 minutes
- Total Time: 5 minutes
Featured Product:

##### Ingredients

- 2 tsps Nescafé Gold Espresso
- 60ml hot water (~80°C)
- 200ml milk of choice
- 20g white chocolate, finely chopped
- Extra grated white chocolate, to finish

##### Equipment

- Mug, to serve
- Hand frother

##### Recipe Steps

- Heat the milk and white chocolate in a small pot, stirring until melted and warmed.​
- Pour the warmed mixture into a jug and froth using a hand frother.​
- Stir 2 tsp of NESCAFE Gold Espresso into 60ml of hot water for a rich espresso base.​
- Pour the warm white chocolate milk and foam into a mug. ​
- Add the bold coffee to the creamy white chocolate milk mixture and watch the flavours come to together.​
- Finish with a shower of white chocolate (as much as you like, we won't tell!) and enjoy.
