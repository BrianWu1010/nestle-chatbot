## NESQUIK Chocolate Syrup 700ml

Wake up your milk with NESQUIK Chocolate syrup - Proudly prepared in Canada and made with no artificial flavours, colours or sweeteners. It’s also enriched with iron to promote the maintenance of your child’s good health. Enjoy as part of a balanced breakfast or afternoon snack, with the taste your child will love.
