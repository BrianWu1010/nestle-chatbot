Whether you're planning for trick-or-treaters or selecting treats for Halloween gatherings, we understand the importance of finding options that suit a variety of dietary needs. NESTLÉ® TURTLES® are made with only quality, natural flavours and are gluten-free. SMARTIES products contain wheat, and some AERO® products may contain wheat. To enjoy your Halloween treats without worry, we recommend always checking the product label for allergen warnings. If a product contains sources of gluten, they will be listed on the package. Product labels are the most up-to-date source of information, and consumers with dietary restrictions should review ingredients before purchasing or consuming products. Only specific products will carry the "Gluten-Free" logo, as we manufacture products in multiple facilities. If your product does not display the logo, it may not meet the requirements to be labeled as gluten-free. We follow Health Canada regulations and CFIA guidance. At Nestlé, sources of gluten are declared by grain name in the ingredient list or, where applicable, in a "May Contain" statement.
As families prepare for Halloween celebrations and seasonal treat sharing, we understand that consumers may have specific dietary or religious considerations. We're always striving to meet the needs of our diverse consumer group. For our Nestlé products sold in Canada, we currently don't facilitate halal certification; instead, we rely on the home market of our imported products for that claim. If a product is already certified, we then determine whether to display the claim in accordance with Canadian Food Inspection Agency guidelines. We always recommend that consumers verify product packaging for specific halal symbols before purchasing or enjoying Halloween treats.
Made in a Peanut-Free Facility For families handing out treats this Halloween, all NESTLÉ KITKAT, AERO, SMARTIES, and COFFEE CRISP mini-sized products are prepared in a Canadian peanut-free facility, where we employ rigorous quality control procedures to maintain peanut-free status. Our Halloween mini-sized favourites, including KITKAT, COFFEE CRISP, AERO, and SMARTIES, proudly display the "Peanut Free" logo and are made in our peanut-free facility in Toronto. Some products produced in other manufacturing locations may not display this logo. If your treat does not show the "Peanut Free" logo on the packaging, it may not have been tested or produced at our peanut-free facility. As always, consumers should review packaging before distributing or consuming Halloween treats. Any products containing peanuts would have them listed in the ingredient list or in a "May Contain" statement in accordance with Health Canada and CFIA regulations. NESTLÉ TURTLES® products contain tree nuts.
Looking for vegetarian-friendly treats this Halloween? NESTLÉ AERO®, TURTLES®, and KITKAT® products are vegetarian, although they do contain dairy ingredients. As formulations can vary, we recommend consumers read the label every time. To enjoy Halloween treats without worry, always verify the product label for allergen warnings and ingredient information. Any of Canada's priority allergens or sensitivities will be clearly listed. The package label is the most up-to-date source of information and should be reviewed before purchase or consumption.
