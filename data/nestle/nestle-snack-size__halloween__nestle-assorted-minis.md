## NESTLÉ Assorted Minis

All Your NESTLÉ favourites in one bag! This assorted pack of perfectly portioned and individually wrapped mini bars and candies is ideal for Halloween, parties, snacking, and sharing with family and friends. Enjoy KITKAT’s crisp wafers and smooth milk chocolate; AERO’s melt-in-your-mouth bites of real milk chocolate filled with tiny bubbles; SMARTIES’ colourful candy-coated milk chocolate; and COFFEE CRISP’s crispy wafer, coffee crème filling, and thin chocolatey coating. Proudly prepared in Canada in a peanut-free facility using quality ingredients, these peanut-free treats are made with natural flavours and no artificial colours.
- The Perfect Treat For Halloween: This assorted pack includes your favourite NESTLÉ mini bars and candies, all perfectly portioned and individually wrapped, making them ideal for Halloween
- All Your Favourites In One Bag: Have your favourite, or all your favourites, in this assorted pack of mini bars and candies, including NESTLÉ KITKAT, AERO, SMARTIES, and COFFEE CRISP
- Made In A Peanut-Free Facility: NESTLÉ KITKAT, AERO, SMARTIES, and COFFEE CRISP minis are prepared in a Canadian peanut-free facility where rigorous quality control processes are employed to ensure its peanut-free status; There may be other products under the KITKAT, AERO, SMARTIES, or COFFEE CRISP brands that contain peanuts, so be sure to read the label every time
- Individually Wrapped: Each treat in this assorted pack is individually wrapped and ready for Halloween, parties, snacking or sharing with family and friends
- Rainforest Alliance Certified: As part of the NESTLÉ Cocoa Plan, NESTLÉ purchases 100% Rainforest Alliance certified cocoa which helps farmers improve the quality of their products and strengthen their communities
- Proudly Supporting Kids Help Phone: For more than 25 years NESTLÉ Canada has raised millions for Kids Help Phone, Canada's only 24/7 e-mental health service, available in English and French
