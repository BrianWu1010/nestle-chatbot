## NESTLÉ Scary Assorted Halloween Full-Sized Bars Carton 8 pack

Celebrate Halloween with NESTLÉ Scary assorted full-size bars and candies! Individually wrapped in scary Halloween packaging, these KITKAT, AERO, SMARTIES, and COFFEE CRISP treats are ideal for Halloween. Treat your trick-or-treaters to KITKAT’s crisp wafers and smooth milk chocolate; AERO's melt-in-your-mouth bites of real milk chocolate filled with tiny bubbles; SMARTIES’ colourful candy-coated milk chocolate; and COFFEE CRISP’s crispy wafer, coffee crème filling, and thin chocolatey coating and master the holiday!
