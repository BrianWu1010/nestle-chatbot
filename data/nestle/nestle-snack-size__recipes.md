### Frequently Asked Questions

A KITKAT 4-Finger Wafer Bar (45 g) contains 230 calories, while a snack-size KITKAT Mini (11.8g) contains 60 calories. A box of SMARTIES (45 g) contains 210 calories. An AERO Milk Chocolate Bar (42g) contains 220 calories. A COFFEE CRISP Wafer Bar (50g) contains 260 calories.

KITKAT 4-Finger Wafer Bar (45 g)
Ingredients: Milk chocolate (sugar, modified milk ingredients, cocoa butter, unsweetened chocolate, soy lecithin, polyglycerol polyricinoleate, natural flavour), Wheat flour, Sugar, Modified palm oil, Cocoa, Baking soda, Soy lecithin, Yeast, Protease, and Xylanase.
SMARTIES (45 g)
Ingredients: Milk chocolate (sugar, milk ingredients, cocoa butter, cocoa mass, whey powder, lactose, soya lecithin, polyglycerol polyricinoleate, natural flavour), Sugar, Wheat flour, Modified corn starch, Carnauba wax, and Colour. Please note that small candies may pose a choking hazard.
COFFEE CRISP Wafer Bar (50 g)
Ingredients: Sugar, Wheat flour, Modified milk ingredients,Palm oil, Modified palm and vegetable oils, Cocoa butter, Cocoa mass, Soy lecithin, Coffee, Cocoa, Baking soda, Yeast, Polyglycerol polyricinoleate, and Natural flavours.
AERO Milk Chocolate Bar (42 g)
Ingredients: Milk chocolate (sugar, modified milk ingredients, cocoa butter, cocoa mass, soy lecithin, polyglycerol polyricinoleate, and natural flavour).

We follow the regulations by Health Canada and guidance from the CFIA. At Nestlé, the sources of gluten are declared by the grain name in the ingredient list or if there is a risk of cross-contamination, in the “May Contain” statement of the product label.
