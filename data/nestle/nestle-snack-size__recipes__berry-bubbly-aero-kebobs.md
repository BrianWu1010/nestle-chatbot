#### Berry Bubbly AERO Kebob Recipe 182g

A fresh and fun idea perfect for a lunchbox treat. Try our berry bubbly kebobs with AERO milk chocolate.
- Serves: 1
- Preparation: 2 minutes
- Cooking: 0 minutes
- Total Time: 2 minutes
Featured Product

##### Ingredients

- 5 AERO Milk Chocolate Minis
- 5 fresh strawberries (or any berry of choice)

##### Equipment

- Kebob skewers or straws (to assemble and hold)

##### Recipe Steps

- Cut top off fresh strawberries. ​
- Begin assembling strawberries and AERO minis onto the skewer, placing a strawberry in the center, and switching between fruit and chocolate. ​
- Finish assembly and enjoy!
