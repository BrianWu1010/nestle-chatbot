#### Chocolatey KitKat Pizza Recipe 8x11.8g

Turn pizza night into dessert with a Chocolatey KITKAT Pizza topped with hazelnut spread, chopped KITKAT and toasted hazelnuts.
- Serves: 8 (78.8g/serving)
- Preparation: 15 minutes
- Cooking: 15 minutes
- Total Time: 30 minutes
Product Featured:

##### Ingredients

- 1 medium pre-baked pizza crust
- 2 tablespoons butter, melted
- 1/4 cup chocolate hazelnut spread
- 4 (4x11.8g) KITKAT Snack-Size Bars
- 2 tablespoons hazelnuts, chopped and toasted

##### Equipment

- Fork
- Spatula
- Oven
- Baking sheet
- Parchment paper

##### Recipe Steps

- Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and preheat oven to 450°F (230°C).
- Place the pizza dough on the baking sheet and brush it with butter.
- Bake pizza dough for 10–15 minutes, or until golden brown.
- Spread the chocolate hazelnut spread over the warm crust.
- Sprinkle the chopped KITKAT Bar and hazelnuts on top, then cut into pieces and serve. Enjoy your KITKAT Pizza!
