#### KitKat Almond Cookies Recipe 295g

Our KITKAT Almond Cookies Recipe makes buttery almond cookies topped with crunchy KITKAT Minis. An easy homemade chocolatey cookie recipe perfect for sharing.
- Serves: 15 (52.21g/serving)
- Preparation: 15 minutes
- Cooking: 15 minutes
- Waiting Time: 10 minutes
- Total Time: 40 minutes
Featured Product

##### Ingredients

- 1 cup (120g) All-Purpose Flour
- ½ cup (60g) Almond Flour or finely ground almonds
- ½ teaspoon (2.5g) Baking Soda
- 1/8 teaspoon Salt
- 1 stick (1/2 cup, 60g), Softened Unsalted Butter
- 1/3 cup (41.6g) Sugar
- ½ teaspoon 2.46ml) Almond Extract
- 2 Large Eggs, divided
- 8, cut into half KitKat Minis

##### Equipments

- Parchment paper
- Baking tray
- Large bowl
- Hand blender or spatula, for mixing

##### Recipe Steps

- Preheat oven to 350F. Line a baking tray with parchment paper.
- Combine all-purpose flour, almond flour, baking soda and salt in mixing bowl. Stir to mix.
- Combine butter and sugar in medium-sized mixing bowl. Using a hand blender or a spatula, beat until it is fluffy and lighter in colour, about 1 minute.
- Add the almond extract and 1 egg. Beat again for a few seconds, until combined.
- Add the dry flour mixture, half at a time, beating well to combine after each addition.
- Scrape out the cookie dough from the blades of the hand mixer.
- Gently knead and combine the dough in the bowl until it comes together, about 30 seconds.
- Divide the dough into 15 - 16 equal pieces. With dampened or lightly greased hands, roll each piece into a smooth ball and place on parchment lined tray.
- Press down gently on each dough ball to flatten lightly.
- Beat remaining egg and brush lightly over the cookies.
- Bake for 15 minutes until cookies are slightly puffed up and golden yellow in colour.
- Remove from oven and gently press in a piece of KitKat Mini over top of each cookie. The warmth of the cookie will anchor the KitKat Mini pieces in place.
- Cool cookies for 10 minutes in baking tray, then transfer to serving platter.
- Store extras in an airtight container for up to two weeks. Enjoy your almond cookies!
