#### KitKat Banana Bread Recipe 295g

Bake moist banana bread muffins loaded with KITKAT chocolatey wafer pieces and crunchy topping as an easy banana bread recipe.
- Serves 1 (942.25g/serving)
- Preparation: 15 minutes
- Cooking Time: 15 minutes
- Total Time: 30 minutes
- Waiting Time: 0 minutes
- Presentation Time: 0 minutes
Product Featured:

##### Ingredients

- 4 (4 x 11.8g) Snack-Size KitKat bars
- ¾ cups all-purpose flour
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 ½ tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp nutmeg
- ¾ tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt
- 2 bananas, mashed, ripe
- 2 tbsp milk
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 large egg
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp, unsalted butter, melted

##### Equipment

- Oven
- Muffin liners, for baking
- Large bowl
- Whisk
- Measuring cup

##### Recipe Steps

- Preheat oven to 400 F. Line standard muffin tin.
- In a large bowl combine flour, sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
- In a large glass measuring cup whisk together bananas, milk, oil and eggs. Pour mixture over dry ingredients and stir.
- Add KIT KAT bars and gently toss to combine.
- To make crumb topping combine KIT KAT crumbs, brown sugar, butter and cinnamon.
- Scoop batter evenly into the muffin tray. Top each muffin with 1 teaspoon crumb topping.
- Place in to bake for 15 minutes.
- Remove from oven and cool.
