#### KitKat Witch’s Broomstick for Halloween Recipes 977g

Crispy cereal treats and KITKAT bars transform into magical broomsticks for a fun, shareable Halloween party dessert.
- Serves: 24 (27.99g/serving)
- Preparation: 30 minutes
- Cooking: 10 minutes
- Waiting Time: 60 minutes
- Total Time: 1 hour and 40 minutes
Featured Product

##### Ingredients

- ¼ cup (60 mL) Butter
- 5 cups (1.25L) Mini-Marshmallows
- 2 tsp (10mL) Vanilla Extract
- 6 cups Cereals; Ready-to-Eat (Rice, Puffed)
- 1 cup (250 mL) Milk Chocolate Chips, Melted and Cooled Slightly
- ½ cup (125 mL) White Chocolate Chips, Melted and Cooled Slightly
- 24 (24x11.8g) KitKat Snack-Size Bars

##### Equipments

- Baking Grease
- Baking Sheet
- Large Saucepan
- Spatula, to spread
- Cutting Board
- Scissors, to cut bag
- Chopstick or Teaspoon Handle
- Resealable Bag x2

##### Recipe Steps

- Grease baking sheet; set aside. Chop 6 KITKAT bars.
- Melt butter in large saucepan set over low heat; stir in marshmallows for about 6 minutes or until completely melted. Stir in vanilla. Remove from heat.
- Immediately stir in cereal and chopped KITKAT bars until coated.
- Scrape onto prepared baking sheet; using wet hands or buttered spatula, spread evenly.
- Let cool slightly; transfer to cutting board and trim edges. Cut into 3 long strips. Cut each strip into 12 triangles. Cut a small part from top of each triangle to create a flat edge; make an indent in the top with a chopstick or teaspoon handle.
- Spoon white chocolate into resealable bag; snip bottom corner. Pipe or drizzle remaining milk chocolate and white chocolate over rice cereal treats to resemble bristles.
- Let stand for about 1 hour or until chocolate is set.
