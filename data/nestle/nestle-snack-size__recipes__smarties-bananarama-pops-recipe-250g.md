#### SMARTIES Bananarama Pops Recipe 250g

Frozen banana pops dipped in creamy AERO milk chocolate and coated with crunchy SMARTIES candy for an easy, dessert.
- Serves: 16 (37.56g/serving)
- Preparation: 8 minutes
- Cooking: 2 minutes
- Waiting Time: 120 minutes
- Total Time: 130 minutes
Featured Product

##### Ingredients

- 4 snack-size boxes (4 x 40g) SMARTIES
- 6 snack-size bars (6 x 44g) AERO
- 4 ripe bananas

##### Equipments

- Lollipop sticks or flat stir sticks (to assemble and hold)
- Parchment paper
- Microwave-safe bowl
- Freezer

##### Recipe Steps

- Cut bananas into 4 even-sized pieces.
- Insert a straw or popsicle stick into each piece and place on parchment paper.
- Freeze for 2 hours.
- Place AERO bars in microwave-safe bowl and microwave in 15-second increments, until melted.
- Dip banana pieces in melted chocolate and roll in crushed SMARTIES. Return to freezer to harden, if necessary.
