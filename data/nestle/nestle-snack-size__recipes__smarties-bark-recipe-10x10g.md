#### SMARTIES Bark Recipe 10x10g

Make this easy SMARTIES Chocolatey Bark for a colourful, fun treat. Perfect for parties, sharing, snacking, and celebrating any occasion.
- Serves: 24 (19.56g/serving)
- Preparation: 3 minutes
- Cooking: 5 minutes
- Waiting: 60 minutes
- Presentation: 2 minutes
- Total Time: 1 hour and 10 minutes
Product Featured:

##### Ingredients

- 5 boxes (5 x 10g) SMARTIES Snack-Size
- 1 ½ cups milk chocolate chips
- ¾ dark chocolate chips

##### Equipment

- Microwave-safe bowl
- Microwave
- Wax Paper
- Refrigerator

##### Recipe Steps

- In microwave-safe bowl, combine chocolate chips. Microwave, stirring at 15 second intervals, until melted.
- Spread chocolate into 1/4-inch thick layer onto wax paper covered baking sheet. Sprinkle with SMARTIES.
- Refrigerate 1 hour or until hardened.
- Break into pieces and enjoy!
