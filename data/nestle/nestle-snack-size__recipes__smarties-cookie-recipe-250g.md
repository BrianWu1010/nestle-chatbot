#### SMARTIES Cookie Recipe 250g

Try this yummy, chocolatey cookie recipe using Smarties! A treat of all ages will love.
- Serves 30 (38.79 g/serving)
- Preparation: 15 minutes
- Cooking Tim: 9 minutes
- Total Time: 24 minutes
Product Featured:

##### Ingredients

- 1 ½ cups (25 snack-size boxes) SMARTIES
- 1 cup butter
- 1 ½ cup brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 eggs
- 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon baking powder

##### Equipment

- Oven, to cook
- Large bowl, to mix
- Electric Mixer, to beat
- Baking sheets
- Baking spray, to grease sheet

##### Recipe Steps

- Preheat oven to 375˚F (190˚C). In large bowl, beat softened butter and packed brown sugar with electric mixer until creamy. Beat in vanilla and eggs.
- Beat in flour, baking powder, and salt.
- Drop slightly rounded tablespoons about 2-inches (5 cm) apart onto greased baking sheets. Flatten cookies slightly and press 5 SMARTIES into top of each.
- Bake for 8 to 9 minutes.
- Take out oven, cool cookies, and enjoy!
