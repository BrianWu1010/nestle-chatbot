#### SMARTIES Halloween Cupcakes for Halloween Recipes 977g

Our festive SMARTIES® Halloween Cupcakes recipe makes delicious cupcakes topped with colourful candy and sprinkles as an easy Halloween dessert idea.
- Serves: 9 (97.33g/serving)
- Preparation: 5 minutes
- Cooking: 12 minutes
- Total Time: 17 minutes
Featured Product

##### Ingredients

- 1 carton of Full Cream Sweetened Condensed Milk
- 1 cup of Flour
- ¾ cup of Cocoa Powder
- ½ cup of Milk
- 1tsp Vanilla Extract
- 1 tsp Baking Powder
- 100g of Softened Butter
- 9 snack-size boxes (9 x 10g) of SMARTIES
- 2 tbsp White Vinegar
- Food Dye (colours based on your choosing)
- Chocolate Sprinkles (for decoration)

##### Equipments

- Paper Cupcake Liners
- Oven
- Large Mixing Bowl

##### Recipe Steps

- Mix the wet ingredients before adding the dry ingredients. Bake at 170F for 20 minutes.
- Mix 100g of softened butter, 200g of icing sugar, 1 tbsp of milk and the food colouring.
- Place the SMARTIES and drizzle the chocolate sprinkles on the cupcakes. Enjoy!
