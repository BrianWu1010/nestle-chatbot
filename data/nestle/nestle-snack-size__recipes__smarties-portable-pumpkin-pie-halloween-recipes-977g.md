#### SMARTIES Portable Pumpkin Pie for Halloween Recipes 977g

Enjoy our SMARTIES Portable Pumpkin Pie recipe, a no-bake fall dessert layered with pumpkin, cream cheese, and ginger snap crumbs in mason jars.
- Serves: 4 (406.24/serving)
- Preparation: 20 minutes
- Total Time: 20 minutes
Featured Product:

##### Ingredients

- 1 box (1x10g) SMARTIES Snack-Size
- 3 cups Ginger Snap Crumbs
- 1 can Pumpkin Puree
- 2 tbsp Brown Sugar
- 1tsp Cinnamon
- 2 cups Low-Fat Whipped Topping
- 8 ounces Cream Cheese

##### Equipment

- Mini-mason jars x4
- Electric Hand Mixer
- Spoon, for layering

##### Recipe Steps

- Lay out 4 mini-mason jars. Make the pumpkin layer by mixing puree, brown sugar and cinnamon
- Make the creamy layer by whipping the cream cheese in a mixer, then folding in the whipped topping.
- Spoon the ginger snap crumbs, the pumpkin layer and the creamy layer into the mini-mason jars, alternating sides.
- End with a dollop of the cream layer, then garnish with crushed SMARTIES.
- Put a lid on the jars and head out on your fall adventure!
