#### SMARTIES Stop Signs Recipe 10x10g

Create colourful SMARTIES Stop Signs with cookies and frosting—a fun, no-bake treat perfect for parties and family activities.
- Serves: 12 (49.58/serving)
- Preparation: 15minutes
- Waiting: 30 minutes
- Total Time: 45 minutes
Product Featured:

##### Ingredients

- 5 (5 x 10g) SMARTIES Snack-Size boxes
- 12 fig cookies
- 2 tablespoons vanilla frosting

##### Equipment

- Refrigerator
- Paper straws or flat stir sticks (for light poles)

##### Recipe Steps

- Start making the ‘light’, by taking your choice of cookie and scoop three vertical dabs of vanilla frosting on the front of the biscuit. Place each of your three SMARTIES stop light colours on the dabs.
- Set cookies in fridge to allow SMARTIES to stick to frosting (approximately 30 minutes).
- Once your SMARTIES lights are set into frosting, it's time to build the 'post section of the stop light. (Paper straws work best for this section, but a flat 'stir stick' can work, too) The straws will be inserted into the filling of your cookie. Before inserting the straw into the filling, flatten the top of the straw about 1-2 inches - this action makes it easier to insert the straw into the fill without breaking the cookie.
- Carefully push your straw into the fig or vanilla to your desire 'light post' length. Be gentle! Pushing the straw too hard will push the SMARTIES off of the frosting. You've just made a SMARTIES stop light. Enjoy.
