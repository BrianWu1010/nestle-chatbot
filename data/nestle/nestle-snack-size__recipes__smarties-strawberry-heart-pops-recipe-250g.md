#### SMARTIES Strawberry Heart Pops Recipe 250g

Make adorable SMARTIES Strawberry Heart Pops with yogurt and fresh strawberries with this recipe—a fun frozen treat for celebrations.
- Serves 24 (17.04g/serving)
- Preparation: 10 minutes
- Cooking Time: 0 minutes
- Total Time: 160 minutes
- Waiting Time: 150 minutes
- Presentation Time: 0 minutes
Featured Product

##### Ingredients

- 4 snack-size boxes (4 x 40g) SMARTIES
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 12 strawberries
- 2 tsp honey (or any other sweetener of your choice)

##### Equipments

- 1 baking sheet
- Parchment paper
- Knife, for cutting berries
- Lollipop sticks or flat stir sticks
- Large bowl
- Whisk, for mixing
- Freezer

##### Recipe Steps

- Prep a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Wash the berries and cult them in half, lengthwise. Skewer each berry with a lollipop stick or stir stick.
- Put Greek yogurt in a bowl, add the sweetener, and mix it all up! Dip the berries into the yogurt mixture, then decorate with crushed SMARTIES and whole SMARTIES.
- Place the SMARTIES-fied strawberries onto the baking sheet until you’re all dipped.
- Freeze for 2.5 hours to let the yogurt set, and fall in love with your new favourite treat!
