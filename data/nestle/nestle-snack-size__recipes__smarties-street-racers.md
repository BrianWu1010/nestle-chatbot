#### SMARTIES Street Racers Recipe 237g

SMARTIES fans, start your chocolatey engines! Build fun edible race cars with NESTLE Minis in just 10 minutes.
- Serves: 1 (247.5/serving)
- Preparation: 10 minutes
- Total Time: 10 minutes
Product Featured:

##### Ingredients

- 1 snack-size box (10g) SMARTIES
- 1 snack-size bar (11g) Coffee Crisp
- 1 snack-size bar (11.68g) KitKat
- 1 snack-size bar (7.33g) Aero

##### Recipe Steps

- Use KitKat, Coffee Crisp and Aero minis as the car base. Use frosting to hold car shapes together.
- Attach SMARTIES to car bases as wheels and windshields and enjoy!
