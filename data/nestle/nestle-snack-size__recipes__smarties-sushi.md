#### SMARTIES Sushi Recipe 10x10g

No fish are harmed in the making of this sushi! Make snacking more fun with SMARTIES.
- Serves 12 (47.23 g/serving)
- Preparation: 18 minutes
- Cooking Time: 2 minutes
- Total Time: 20 minutes
Featured Product:

##### Ingredients

- 9 boxes (9x10g) of SMARTIES Snack-Size
- 1 box roll-up fruit snacks
- ¼ cup melted butter (or melted margarine)
- 4 cups mini marshmallows
- 5 cups puffed rice cereal

##### Equipment

- Kebob skewers or straws (to assemble and hold)

##### Recipe Steps

- Melt butter or margarine in microwave
- Add marshmallows and stir constantly until melted. Add cereal & stir until coated
- Spoon of puffed rice mixture into unrolled fruit snacks
- Fill centre with SMARTIES, roll up tightly, and cut 1.5”” pieces and enjoy!

##### Tips

- We used mini KITKAT bars to create nigiri rolls
- Delicious when garnished with fresh fruit
