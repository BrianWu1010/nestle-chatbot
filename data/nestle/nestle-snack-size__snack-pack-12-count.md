## Nestlé Snack Pack

Enjoy NESTLÉ favourites like KITKAT, AERO, SMARTIES, COFFEE CRISP in snack-size bars and candies! Individually wrapped and prepared in a Canadian peanut-free facility, they're safe for school and perfect for lunches. Find this pack in your grocer's Snack Aisle.
Nestlé Snack Size Features and Benefits
- All Your Favourites In One Pack: This snack pack of assorted NESTLÉ favourites -- KITKAT, AERO, SMARTIES, and COFFEE CRISP -- can be found in your grocer's Snack Aisle
- Something for Everyone: Enjoy AERO's melt-in-your-mouth milk chocolate bubbles, COFFEE CRISP's blend of crispy wafers and coffee crème covered with a thin chocolatey coating, KITKAT's crisp wafer, or SMARTIES' colourful candy-coated milk chocolate
- Safe for School: These individually wrapped snack size bars and candies are safe for school and perfect for lunches
- Made In A Peanut-Free Facility: NESTLÉ KITKAT, AERO, and COFFEE CRISP snack size bars and SMARTIES candies are prepared in a Canadian peanut-free facility where rigorous quality control processes are employed to ensure their peanut-free status; There may be other KITKAT, AERO, SMARTIES, or COFFEE CRISP products under the KITKAT, AERO, SMARTIES, or COFFEE CRISP brands that contain peanuts, make sure to read the label every time
- NESTLÉ Cocoa Plan: In collaboration with the Rainforest Alliance, the NESTLÉ Cocoa Plan is helping cocoa farmers enhance farming practices
- Proudly Supporting Kids Help Phone: For more than 34 years, NESTLÉ Canada has raised millions for Kids Help Phone, Canada’s only 24/7, free, confidential, and multilingual e-mental health service
