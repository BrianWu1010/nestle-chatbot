## SMARTIES Easter Chocolate Egg Hunt Kit

Spring into Easter with chocolaty eggs ready for an exciting Easter egg hunt. NESTLÉ SMARTIES Milk Chocolaty Easter Egg Hunt Kit (140 g) is a ready-to-hide set of hollow, milk chocolaty eggs filled with miniature SMARTIES candy-coated milk chocolate pieces. Each individually-wrapped SMARTIES egg is an explosion of colourful, crunchy, shareable delight that's made with natural flavours and non- colours. Whether you're hiding them or filling up an Easter basket, they're a vibrant way to celebrate the Easter season.
