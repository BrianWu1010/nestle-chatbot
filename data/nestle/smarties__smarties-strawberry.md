## SMARTIES Strawberry

SMARTIES is bringing a favourite fruit flavour to the palm of your hands in bit sized pieces. Just in time for summer, NESTLÉ SMARTIES Strawberry (45g) is a limited-edition flavour that combines delicious milk chocolate with strawberry flavour. Each box contains the delicious combination of milk chocolate coated in vibrant red strawberry flavour candy shells. PLUS, they are produced in a peanut-free facility. Smarties Strawberry is easy to share with family and friends and with all of them being red, you'll definitely eat the red ones last!​
Smarties Strawberry Features and Benefits
- SMARTIES Strawberry flavoured candy-coated pieces of milk chocolate​
